# Raise Hand — for facilitators

Raise Hand is a utility that gives the room a fair queue: participants tap ✋,
and you see who's waiting, in order, in the footer of your view. It replaces
the loudest-voice-wins dynamic with a visible, first-come queue — and it works
across whatever tool the group is currently using.

## Samvinas

- Enable Raise Hand in the **Utilities** section of your session view.
- Participants tap the ✋ in their footer to join the queue (and tap again to
  leave it).
- Your footer shows the queue in order. **Next** clears the current speaker
  and moves on; **Clear All** empties the queue at a scene change.

Raise Hand sits outside the flow — no items in, none out; it just manages the
speaking order over whatever the group is doing.

## Paper and pen

You need: a corner of the flip chart (or whiteboard) the room can see, and a
marker within your reach.

- Announce the rule once: "raise your hand — I'll add you to the list and take
  people in order."
- Write names in the corner as hands go up. The visible list is what makes
  the queue trusted — people put their hands down once their name is on it.
- Cross names off as they speak; wipe the list clean between activities.
- Failure mode to avoid: running the queue from memory. The first time you
  skip someone, hands stop going up and shouting resumes.

## When & why

Reach for a queue when contributions come through the air rather than through
the tools — Q&A, report-backs, plenary discussion — especially in large rooms
or online, where the loudest voice otherwise wins.

- Say how you'll use it, once: "if you want to speak, raise your hand in the
  app — I'll take people in order."
- Watch for hands that have waited long: a queue is only fair if it actually
  gets served.
- Clear the queue between activities so stale hands don't carry over.
- If the same few names keep filling the list,
  [Brain Writing](/facilitator/brainwriting.html) or
  [Breakout Groups](/facilitator/breakout-groups.html) spread participation
  further than any queue can.
