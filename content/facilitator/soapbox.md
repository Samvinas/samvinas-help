# Soapbox — for facilitators

Soapbox runs a queue of volunteers for short, uninterrupted talks — typically
two minutes, no slides. It gives people a place to share something interesting
and relevant without disrupting the flow of the event. Run soapboxes just
before or just after breaks.

## How to run it

- Add the tool and frame the ask in the **Prompt** ("Two minutes, no slides —
  what should the group hear about?").
- Participants add themselves (name plus an optional topic) and the queue
  builds in the order people sign up.
- When a slot opens, take speakers from the top — around three fits most
  breaks. Mark each one **Done** as they finish; their entry drops below the
  queue, crossed out, so everyone can see who has spoken.
- The rest of the list carries over to the next slot. There's no guarantee of
  reaching everyone — say so up front.

## Managing the queue

Open the tool's gear menu and use the **Data** tab:

- **Reorder** with the arrows when someone can't go next.
- **Done** when a talk finishes — use **Restore** if you tap it by mistake.
- **Remove** someone who asks to come off the list (they can withdraw
  themselves only from the device they signed up on). Removing takes two
  taps — the ✕ asks "Remove?" before anything happens.

## Facilitation tips

- Put the display view on the big screen — the "Up next" card does the
  introductions for you.
- Hold the no-interruptions rule for the speaker: questions can go to a
  brainstorm or the break.
- The Timer utility makes the two-minute limit visible to the room.
