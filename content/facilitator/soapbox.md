# Soapbox — for facilitators

Soapbox runs a queue of volunteers for short, uninterrupted talks — typically
two minutes, no slides. An **organising** tool for airtime: it gives people a
place to share something interesting and relevant without disrupting the flow
of the event.

## Samvinas

- Add the tool and frame the ask in the **Prompt** ("Two minutes, no slides —
  what should the group hear about?").
- Participants add themselves (name plus an optional topic) and the queue
  builds in the order people sign up.
- When a slot opens, take speakers from the top — around three fits most
  breaks. Mark each one **Done** as they finish; their entry drops below the
  queue, crossed out, so everyone can see who has spoken.
- The rest of the list carries over to the next slot.
- Put the display view on the big screen — the "Up next" card does the
  introductions for you.

To manage the queue, open the tool's gear menu and use the **Data** tab:
**Reorder** with the arrows when someone can't go next; **Done** when a talk
finishes (**Restore** if you tap it by mistake); **Remove** someone who asks to
come off the list — they can withdraw themselves only from the device they
signed up on, and removing takes two taps: the ✕ asks "Remove?" first.

## Paper and pen

You need: a flip chart by the door, a marker on a string, and a visible timer
(a phone propped on the lectern will do).

- Head the sheet with the ask — "Soapbox: two minutes, no slides. Name +
  topic" — and rule a dozen numbered lines.
- People add themselves during breaks; the numbered lines *are* the queue.
- Work from the top when a slot opens; cross each name out as they finish, so
  the room can see who has spoken and who's next.
- Carry the uncrossed names to the next slot — no need to re-copy, just bring
  the sheet.
- The failure mode is the unenforced clock: one five-minute talk and the queue
  dies with the break. Start the timer where the speaker can see it, and call
  time cheerfully but firmly.

## When & why

Reach for Soapbox when the room is full of things worth hearing that don't
belong on the agenda — it organises that energy into slots instead of letting
it leak into interruptions. Run soapboxes just before or just after breaks.

- Hold the no-interruptions rule for the speaker: questions can go to a
  [brainstorm](/facilitator/brainstorm.html) or the break.
- The [Timer](/facilitator/timer.html) utility makes the two-minute limit
  visible to the room.
- There's no guarantee of reaching everyone — say so up front.
- What usually follows: the agenda, refreshed — Soapbox stands outside the
  piped flow. If a talk sparks something, catch it in a
  [Brainstorm](/facilitator/brainstorm.html) rather than a debate.
