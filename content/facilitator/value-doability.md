# Value / Doability — for facilitators

A 2D scoring tool: participants rate each idea on two dimensions and see the
results as a scatter plot. A **convergent** step, ideal for prioritising once
you've generated and grouped ideas.

## Samvinas

- Pipe in the shortlist (often the top-voted ideas).
- Rename the axes in settings if "Value" and "Doability" aren't the right pair
  (e.g. Impact / Effort).
- Give people time — the Submit bar sits at the bottom of the list.

The results view plots every idea on the two axes. Dot colour is agreement, not
score — orange means the room is split, which is usually the more interesting
conversation.

Six numbered dots are placed across the grid; four are blue for high consensus and two orange for wide disagreement. A numbered key below lists each idea with its response count.](/assets/images/screenshots/value-doability-scatter.png)

Value / Doability consumes idea items from an earlier tool — connect its
**Source** and, if you voted first, filter to the items with enough votes.

## Paper and pen

You need: a flip chart with a large 2×2 drawn on it (Doability across the
bottom, Value up the side), the shortlisted ideas on sticky notes, and a pen
per person.

- Take the ideas one at a time. Everyone writes their two scores (1–5 for
  value, 1–5 for doability) on their own paper **before** anyone speaks.
- Call the scores out, then stick the note where the averages put it. If the
  called scores were all over the place, dog-ear the note or mark it with an
  asterisk — that's your "orange dot".
- One failure mode to avoid: placing each note by open discussion. The loudest
  voice drags it to their quadrant; silent scoring first keeps the plot honest.
- Harvest: ring the top-right quadrant, list its contents on a fresh sheet, and
  photograph the whole grid — marked disagreements included.

## When & why

Reach for Value / Doability when a shortlist needs prioritising on more than
gut feel — after generating and a first cut, before committing to action.

> A [convergent](/principles/convergence.html) step. Scores start the
> conversation; they don't end it — the disagreements are usually the useful
> part.

- Discuss the **quadrants**, not individual dots: quick wins vs big bets vs
  not-now.
- Where agreement is low (the colouring shows it), dig in — people may be
  scoring on different assumptions.
- What usually follows: [Elaboration](/facilitator/elaboration.html) or
  [PPCO](/facilitator/ppco.html) to develop the quick wins, or
  [Sign Up](/facilitator/signup.html) to put names against them.
