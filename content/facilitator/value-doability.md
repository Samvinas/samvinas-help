# Value / Doability — for facilitators

A 2D scoring tool: participants rate each idea on two dimensions and see the
results as a scatter plot. Ideal for prioritising once you've generated and
grouped ideas.

> A [convergent](/principles/convergence.html) step. Scores start the conversation;
> they don't end it — the disagreements are usually the useful part.

## How to run it

- Pipe in the shortlist (often the top-voted ideas).
- Rename the axes in settings if "Value" and "Doability" aren't the right pair
  (e.g. Impact / Effort).
- Give people time — the Submit bar sits at the bottom of the list.

The results view plots every idea on the two axes. Dot colour is agreement, not score — orange means the room is split, which is usually the more interesting conversation.

![The Value / Doability results view: a scatter plot with do-ability across the bottom and value up the side, the high-value high-do-ability quadrant tinted green. Six numbered dots are placed across the grid; four are blue for high consensus and two orange for wide disagreement. A numbered key below lists each idea with its response count.](/assets/images/screenshots/value-doability-scatter.png)

## Facilitation tips

- Discuss the **quadrants**, not individual dots: quick wins vs big bets vs
  not-now.
- Where agreement is low (the colouring shows it), dig in — people may be scoring
  on different assumptions.
