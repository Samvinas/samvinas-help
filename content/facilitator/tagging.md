# Tagging — for facilitators

Tagging sorts piped items into facilitator-defined buckets (e.g.
short/medium/long-term, or by theme). An **organising** tool: it can run as a
personal sort or as a group activity that shows consensus.

## Samvinas

- Pipe in the items and define your categories in settings.
- Choose single-user (personal sorting) or multi-user (group consensus) to fit
  the activity.

Tagging consumes any piped items — ideas, actions, clusters — and the
categories you define are yours to name, so it bends to whatever sort the
moment needs.

## Paper and pen

You need: the items on sticky notes or index cards, masking tape, a fat
marker, and wall space.

- Divide the wall into labelled columns with masking tape — one heading per
  category, written big.
- Move each item into a column. For a group sort, do it as a gallery: anyone
  may move any note, and a note that keeps bouncing between columns gets a red
  dot — that's your consensus view, marking what the group reads differently.
- For a personal sort, deal each person their own set of cards and let them
  make columns on the table before comparing.
- The failure mode is the catch-all column — "other" or "later" quietly
  swallows the sort. If a column is filling faster than the rest, its category
  is too broad; split it or sharpen the definitions.
- Harvest: photograph each column with its heading legible, and list the
  red-dotted items — they're the discussion agenda.

## When & why

Reach for Tagging when the group needs items **sorted, not shortlisted** —
everything keeps its place, it just gains a category (time horizon, theme,
owner-to-be).

- Keep categories few and clearly distinct.
- The consensus view highlights items the group sees differently — a good
  place to focus discussion.
- What usually follows: [Voting](/facilitator/voting.html) or
  [Ranking](/facilitator/ranking.html) within a category, or
  [Sign Up](/facilitator/signup.html) once a sort by time horizon has made the
  near-term work visible.
