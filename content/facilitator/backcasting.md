# Backcasting — for facilitators

Backcasting starts from a **desired future** and works backwards: the group
pictures success and asks "for that to be true, what must have happened just
before — and before that?" — laying the steps onto a rough timeline from now
to the goal. An **organising** tool: it sequences without cutting anything.

## Samvinas

- First, help the group converge on the **desired future state**, then run a
  brainstorm — "In order to have achieved this, what must have happened?" —
  and optionally tidy the steps with Voting or Clustering.
- Pipe those steps into Backcasting. In its settings, paste the **desired
  future state** (it shows as the anchor at the top) and set the number of
  time blocks if ten isn't right.
- Work through the steps with the whole group. A good prompt: "What are the
  immediate antecedents of the future state?" — those go in block 9. Then
  work back. Each step has a dropdown (0 = earliest, 9 = just before the
  future, or "–" to leave it unplaced); anyone can set or change any step,
  and everyone sees the board update live. Unplaced steps sit in a pool at
  the top.

![The Backcasting board: four numbered blocks left to right, labelled 0 "earliest" through 3 "nearest the future", each holding one step. Reading right to left gives the working-backwards order; reading left to right gives the plan.](/assets/images/screenshots/backcasting-board.png)

Because each step is tagged with its block, you can **pipe a single block
onward**: send all the "0" steps into a [Sign Up](/facilitator/signup.html)
tool to find owners, or the "5" steps into
[Elaboration](/facilitator/elaboration.html) to flesh them out — add the
downstream tool, pipe from Backcasting, and pick the block in the pipe's
filter.

## Paper and pen

You need: a long wall, masking tape, sticky notes, fat markers, and one big
sheet for the future state.

- Tape a timeline along the wall. The future-state sheet goes at the far
  right end; "now" at the left. Divide the line into blocks with short
  cross-strips of tape and number them.
- Brainstorm the steps onto stickies first — one step per note, written big.
- Place them **from the future end backwards**: stand at the future-state
  sheet and ask "what must have happened just before this?" Post, then repeat
  for the block before. Anyone may move any sticky.
- Keep an unplaced pool on a flip chart beside the wall — not every step has
  to find a block.
- Failure mode: drifting into working *forwards* from today. That collapses
  into ordinary next-steps planning and the future state loses its pull.
  Physically start each round at the right-hand end.
- Harvest: write its block number on each sticky, then photograph the whole
  wall before anything comes down.

## When & why

Reach for Backcasting near the end of the process, when the group has a goal
that feels distant and "what should we do next?" is producing only the
obvious. Working backwards from success frees people from the constraints of
the present — see where planning for action sits in the
[four stages](/principles/four-stages.html).

- Keep it rough — you're after the shape of the sequence, not exact timing.
  It's a sense of order, not a project plan.
- Not every step has to be placed; the unplaced pool is fine to leave.
- What usually follows: [Sign Up](/facilitator/signup.html) to put owners on
  the earliest steps, or [Elaboration](/facilitator/elaboration.html) to
  flesh out the ones that matter most.
