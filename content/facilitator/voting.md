# Voting — for facilitators

Voting is a fast **convergent** tool: a star toggle that surfaces the group's
favourites in real time. A first cut, not a final decision.

## Samvinas

- Pipe in the items to vote on (usually from Brainstorm or Clustering).
- Set the **max votes per person** in settings — a good rule of thumb is about a
  third of the number of items.
- Put a clear criterion in the **Prompt** ("Which three would have the biggest
  impact?").
- Watch the live ranking; **Lock** when voting's done.

Voting adds vote counts to items, so a downstream tool can filter by a minimum
number of votes (e.g. only items with two or more votes flow into Value /
Doability).

## Paper and pen

You need: sheets of dot stickers (three to five per person), and the wall of
numbered sticky notes from the previous activity.

- Number the notes if they aren't already — dots need addresses.
- Say the criterion out loud and write it above the wall.
- Hand out the dots — about a third as many as there are items — and let
  everyone vote **at the same time**, milling at the wall. Stacking several
  dots on one favourite is allowed; say so.
- One failure mode to avoid: voting one person at a time. Watching dots
  accumulate herds the room — get everyone deciding before anyone approaches
  the wall, then vote in a scrum.
- Harvest: write each item's dot count on its note, pull the leaders onto a
  fresh flip chart, and photograph the whole wall before anything moves.

## When & why

Reach for Voting straight after a generating tool, when the field is wide and
the group needs a quick, low-stakes way to narrow it.

> Converging well is a skill of its own — see
> [convergence](/principles/convergence.html) for affirmative-first choosing and
> why you keep the novel option alive a little longer than feels comfortable.

- Say the criterion out loud — people vote very differently for "most exciting"
  vs "most feasible."
- Voting is a temperature check, not a verdict. Use it to narrow the field, then
  discuss the top items.
- What usually follows: a criteria-rich focusing tool —
  [Value / Doability](/facilitator/value-doability.html) or
  [Ranking](/facilitator/ranking.html) — or straight into
  [Elaboration](/facilitator/elaboration.html) if the winners are clear.
