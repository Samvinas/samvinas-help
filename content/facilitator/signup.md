# Sign Up — for facilitators

Sign Up turns a list into commitments: participants add their names to the
items they'll carry forward. An **organising** tool — nothing is cut, but every
item gains (or conspicuously lacks) an owner. A clean way to close a session.

## Samvinas

- Pipe in the items (actions, tasks, ideas to develop).
- Frame the ask in the **Prompt** ("Which will you take the lead on?").
- Export the results so owners have a record after the session.

Sign Up consumes whatever items you pipe in — it works as well on a shortlist
from a focusing tool as on a set of named clusters.

## Paper and pen

You need: one flip-chart sheet (or A4) per item, fat markers, and tape.

- Write one item at the top of each sheet and post them around the room, well
  spaced.
- Run it as a gallery walk: people circulate and write their name on the
  sheets they'll take on. More than one name per sheet is fine — that's a team
  forming.
- Give it a real time-box and stay out of the way — hovering turns
  commitment into compliance.
- The failure mode is vague interest masquerading as ownership. Frame the ask
  as taking the lead, not "sounds interesting" — a name on the sheet is a
  promise, and everyone should hear that before the pens come out.
- Harvest: read every sheet aloud — names and orphans alike — then photograph
  them and let owners carry their sheets away. The orphaned items deserve a
  deliberate decision: drop, defer, or find a home.

## When & why

Reach for Sign Up at the close, when the group has chosen what to do and the
question left is **who**. Ownership claimed in the room, in front of peers,
outlives ownership assigned in a follow-up email.

- Read the sign-ups aloud to make commitments visible and real.
- Items with no names are information, not failure — better to learn now that
  nothing has an owner than three weeks later.
- More than one person on an item is a feature: pairs start faster than
  soloists.
- What usually follows: [CSV Export](/facilitator/csv-export.html) so the
  commitments leave the room in writing, or the
  [Event Report](/facilitator/event-report.html) to close out the day.
