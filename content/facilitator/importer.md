# Importer — for facilitators

The Importer is where an event's starting material goes. When the ideas
already exist — from a previous session, a survey, a document, a KIStorm
export — paste them here and let the rest of the flow work on them, instead
of asking participants to brainstorm a list you already have.

It deliberately has no way to add ideas on screen. Everything arrives
through one door: the import box.

## How to use it

- Add **Importer** to your flow, usually as the first tool.
- It starts **hidden from participants** — that's on purpose. It's a supply
  cupboard, not an activity; participants see its ideas through whichever
  tool you connect to it.
- Open the tool's gear menu, go to the **Data** tab, and tap
  **Import items…**. Paste your list — one idea per line — and import.
- The **Contents** list above the button shows what landed. Tap an item's
  text to fix a typo; the ⋯ menu deletes a stray line.
- Connect the next tool (Voting, Clustering, PPCO…) to the Importer as its
  source — tools added straight after it connect automatically — and the
  pasted ideas flow downstream like any brainstormed ones.

## Tips

- One line becomes one idea, so tidy the list in your notes app first —
  strip numbering, headers, and blank padding.
- Want participants to see the raw list as a reference page? Flip the tool
  visible in its **Settings** tab. They'll get a read-only view — looking is
  fine, adding still goes through you.
- Importing again appends everything you paste — including lines that are
  already there. Adding more later? Paste just the new lines, then glance at
  Contents.
