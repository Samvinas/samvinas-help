# Facilitator guide

Everything here is for the person **running** a Samvinas event. (If you're
taking part in one instead, you don't need any of this — and you'll find
your own guide behind the **?** inside whichever tool you're using.)

These pages assume you already know your way around a creative process: they
show you how Samvinas works and jog your memory on the method, rather than
teaching it from scratch.

## Set up your first event

- **[Setting up an event](/facilitator/getting-started.html)** — the
  mechanics, end to end: create an event, build a flow, pipe one tool into
  another, get people in, get the results out.

## Design and facilitate

- **[Option Explorer](/facilitator/option-explorer.html)** — choosing a

- **[Facilitating the room](/facilitator/facilitating.html)** — six habits

- **[Principles refresher](/principles/)** — diverge and converge, the four

## The toolbox

Tools are grouped the way the app's picker groups them — by the kind of move
they make:

- **[Generating tools](/facilitator/tools-generating.html)** — widen the
  field: produce many options before any judging starts.
- **[Focusing tools](/facilitator/tools-focusing.html)** — narrow down:
  compare, weigh and choose among what you have.
- **[Organising tools](/facilitator/tools-organising.html)** — make sense:
  group, map, sequence and develop, without cutting anything.

A well-shaped session alternates between them: diverge with a generating
tool, then organise or converge with what came out — and repeat.

### Shaping the event

Three tools shape the session rather than the ideas in it:

- **[Breakout Groups](/facilitator/breakout-groups.html)** — split into
  small groups, each with a shared workspace.
- **[Personal Workspace](/facilitator/workspace-launcher.html)** — give
  every participant their own mini-flow.
- **[Instructions](/facilitator/instructions.html)** — a page of guidance in
  the flow.

### Utilities

Enable these once and they are available across every tool in the event:

- **[Timer](/facilitator/timer.html)** — a shared countdown in everyone's
  footer, with fullscreen presenter mode on any device.
- **[Raise Hand](/facilitator/raise-hand.html)** — a fair, visible speaking
  queue.
- **[Event Info](/facilitator/event-info.html)** — a standing page for WiFi,
  schedule and venue details.

### Getting your data out

- **[The event report](/facilitator/event-report.html)** — the whole event
  as one shareable HTML file, formatted for reading.
- **[CSV export](/facilitator/csv-export.html)** — every tool's results as a
  spreadsheet-ready file.
