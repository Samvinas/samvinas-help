# Rich Brainstorm — for facilitators

A richer Brainstorm: multiline Markdown plus a freehand `/sketch` canvas. Use
it when ideas need more than a single line — concepts, sketches, structured
notes. Still **divergent**; the ideas are just bigger.

## Samvinas

- Set an open **Prompt**, as with Brainstorm.
- Let people know they can format with Markdown and sketch by typing
  `/sketch` — many won't discover it unprompted.
- Otherwise run it like Brainstorm: keep it open, encourage quantity, lock it
  when you're ready to converge.

Rich Brainstorm produces `idea` items like any brainstorm; consuming tools
(Voting, Ranking) show a "more" button, so rich ideas stay readable when you
converge.

## Paper and pen

You need: A5 sheets or large index cards, fat markers and fine pens, and
Blu-Tack or tape for a wall gallery.

- One concept per card: a **title in fat marker**, then a sketch or a few
  lines of detail in fine pen — readable at arm's length.
- Timebox each card at three or four minutes, or people polish drawings
  instead of having the next idea. Rough is the point.
- As cards finish, post them on the wall; each author gives a one-sentence
  tour of theirs.
- Harvest: number the cards and photograph the gallery — the cards themselves
  become the units for dot-voting or clustering by hand.

## When & why

Reach for Rich Brainstorm when a one-line idea can't carry the content —
design problems, systems, anything visual or structural. If speed and volume
matter more than depth, plain [Brainstorm](/facilitator/brainstorm.html) is
the better tool.

> Same rules as any [divergent](/principles/divergence.html) step: quantity
> first, judgement later.

- A rough sketch often says more than a paragraph — say so out loud, or
  people will aim for polish.
- Expect fewer items than a plain brainstorm; each one carries more.
- What usually follows: [Voting](/facilitator/voting.html) or
  [Value / Doability](/facilitator/value-doability.html) to converge, or
  [Elaboration](/facilitator/elaboration.html) to develop the keepers further.
