# Event Info — for facilitators

Event Info is a standing page inside your event for the practical details
people keep asking about: WiFi credentials, the day's schedule, room
locations, lunch arrangements. Write it once; participants find it in their
menu whenever they need it, without interrupting the current activity.

## Samvinas

- Enable Event Info in the **Utilities** section of your session view.
- Write the content in Markdown — headings, lists and links all work.
- Rename the page's label if you like, and add more than one instance when a
  single page would get long (say, *Schedule* and *Venue*).
- Participants open it from their menu at any time; it doesn't take part in
  the flow or need Focus.

Event Info sits outside the flow — it produces no items and consumes none; it
is simply always there in the participants' menu.

## Paper and pen

You need: a flip chart, a fat marker, and the wall by the door.

- Write the standing facts once, before people arrive: WiFi and password at
  the top (the first thing anyone asks), then timings, rooms, meals.
- Post it where people naturally pause — by the door, by the coffee — not
  behind you at the front.
- Update it in marker as things change; cross out rather than erase, so the
  room can see the schedule moved.
- Failure mode to avoid: answering the same question aloud all day. Point at
  the sheet instead — that's how the room learns where answers live.

## When & why

Reach for Event Info for anything you'd otherwise put on a flip-chart by the
door: WiFi and passwords · schedule and timings · rooms and maps · meals and
dietary notes · emergency contacts.

- Writing it down once buys back the interruptions — practical questions stop
  arriving mid-activity.
- For **task** instructions tied to a step in the work, use the
  [Instructions](/facilitator/instructions.html) flow tool instead — Event
  Info is for the standing facts of the event.
