# LCO — for facilitators

LCO is an affirmative way to react to an option: the group notes what they
**Like**, raises **Concerns** as questions, and captures the **Opportunities**
the option opens up. An **organising** tool on the same engine as
[PPCO](/facilitator/ppco.html) — but unlike PPCO or ALoU, it doesn't answer
its concerns in place. The Opportunities feed the next round of ideation.

## Samvinas

- Pipe in the ideas you want to react to. Each becomes a card with three
  sections:
  - **Likes** — what appeals about it.
  - **Concerns** — worries, stated as questions ("How to…?").
  - **Opportunities** — new possibilities or directions it suggests.
- Do the Likes first and genuinely — the affirmative start is the point.

LCO consumes `idea` items; pipe it onward into a fresh
[Brainstorm](/facilitator/brainstorm.html) to turn the opportunities (and
the open questions) into new ideas.

## Paper and pen

You need: a flip-chart sheet per option, fat markers, sticky notes, and a
blank wall for the next round.

- Rule each sheet into three columns — **Likes**, **Concerns**,
  **Opportunities** — with the option written across the top.
- Take the columns strictly in order, one sticky per point, said aloud as it
  goes up. Likes must come first, and be genuine.
- Concerns go up only as questions ("How to…?"); rewrite verdicts before
  posting.
- Treat the Opportunities column as the harvest: lift those stickies onto the
  blank wall as seed notes, write the strongest as fresh "How might we…?"
  prompts, and open the next brainstorm from there.
- Failure mode: letting the session become a verdict on the option. LCO is a
  springboard — if the group starts thumbs-up/thumbs-down, point them back at
  the Opportunities column.
- Photograph the sheets before anything moves.

## When & why

Reach for LCO in the **develop** stage when an option needs building rather
than filtering — or when the group's first instinct is to attack. Starting
positive on purpose changes the posture: people engage more when their first
move is to build.

> Concerns phrased as questions become springboards, not dead ends — see
> [problems as questions](/principles/problems-as-questions.html).

- Do the Likes first and genuinely — the affirmative start is the point.
- Treat Opportunities as the real payoff: they are where the next ideas come
  from.
- What usually follows: a fresh [Brainstorm](/facilitator/brainstorm.html)
  seeded by the opportunities, then a focusing tool such as
  [Voting](/facilitator/voting.html) once the new field is open.
