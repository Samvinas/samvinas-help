# Timer — for facilitators

The Timer is a utility, not a flow tool: enable it once and a shared countdown
appears in the sticky footer of everyone's screen, across every tool in your
event. Timeboxes are half of good facilitation — a visible clock does the
nagging so you don't have to.

## Samvinas

- Enable the Timer in the **Utilities** section of your session view.
- Set a duration and start it. Everyone — participants, the display page, you —
  sees the same countdown in the footer.
- **Pause** and **Reset** are yours; Reset returns the clock to the duration
  you configured, ready to start again.
- The panel remembers your last duration, and you can type a custom number of
  minutes.
- Any device can turn the countdown into a full-screen clock — tap the footer
  chip, anywhere along it, including the small expand icon at its right-hand
  end. Useful for a laptop feeding a projector, or a spare tablet propped on
  a table. Fullscreen is a local view (each device chooses its own), the
  screen stays awake, and your pause/reset controls are in the overlay.

The Timer sits outside the flow — it produces no items and consumes none; it
simply keeps time over whatever tool the group is in.

## Paper and pen

You need: a kitchen timer or a phone with a big timer display, and somewhere
the whole room can see it.

- Write the **end time**, not the duration, on the flip chart — "until 14:25"
  keeps working even when nobody is watching a clock.
- Prop the timer where it can be seen, or project a phone timer if there's a
  screen.
- Give one spoken waypoint — "two minutes left" — not a running commentary.
- Failure mode to avoid: keeping the time in your head. If only you know the
  deadline, only you feel it — and the nagging becomes your job again.

## When & why

Reach for a visible timebox for any bounded burst of work — a brainstorm
round, a breakout task, a report-back — anywhere energy leaks when the end is
vague.

- Announce the timebox *before* starting the clock, and say what happens when
  it ends — "three minutes, then we vote" beats a countdown with no
  consequence.
- Short beats generous: a slightly-too-tight timebox keeps energy up, and you
  can always add a minute.
- What usually follows: whatever you promised — a focusing tool like
  [Voting](/facilitator/voting.html), or the report-back from
  [Breakout Groups](/facilitator/breakout-groups.html).
