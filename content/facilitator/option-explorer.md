# Option Explorer — choosing a design for your event

You know an event needs to happen — the group, the problem, perhaps even the
date are set — but not yet what shape the session should take. This page is
for that moment. It won't hand you a script; it helps you make the two
choices a design comes down to: **where to enter the process, and which
tools to enter it with.**

## Start from what the group already has

Sessions rarely start at the beginning. **You enter the process wherever
your group's work already is**, and finding that point is quicker than it
sounds:

- **[The process at a glance](/principles/process-at-a-glance.html)** lays
  the six steps side by side. Read the *Start here when…* column until a row
  sounds like your situation — that's your entry point.
- **[The four stages](/principles/four-stages.html)** is the map behind the
  table: what each stage is for, and what "done" looks like in each.

## Turn the step into a flow

Once you know the step, the flow largely designs itself, because every step
has the same heartbeat: **widen the field first, then make sense of what you
got.** Pick one tool from each side:

- **[Generating tools](/facilitator/tools-generating.html)** widen — many
  ideas, options and possibilities before any judging starts.
- **[Focusing tools](/facilitator/tools-focusing.html)** narrow — compare,
  weigh and choose among what the group produced.
- **[Organising tools](/facilitator/tools-organising.html)** make sense —
  group, map and sequence without cutting anything.

A session usually chains two or three of these widen-then-narrow pairs.
Resist the urge to add more: **a good design has fewer steps than you
think**, each given more time than feels comfortable.

## The classic shape

If you're unsure, start here: **Brainstorm → Clustering → Voting.** Generate
widely, group what emerged so the room can see its own thinking, then choose
together. It fits in an hour, it works with six people or sixty, and every
part of it is forgiving — whatever the group produces, the next step can use.

Then vary it as the situation demands: swap
[Brainstorm](/facilitator/brainstorm.html) for
[Brain Writing](/facilitator/brainwriting.html) when a few loud voices are
likely to dominate; follow the vote with [PPCO](/facilitator/ppco.html) to
strengthen the winning idea rather than merely picking it; put
[Five Whys](/facilitator/five-whys.html) in front of everything when the
group isn't yet sure it's solving the right problem.

## Or start from a template

A **template** is a saved flow. When you create an event you can start from
one instead of a blank flow — a design you've saved yourself, or one your
organisation shares with everyone. And it works in both directions: when you
build a session that works, **save it as a template** (the **⋯** menu at the
top right of the session view) so the design is there waiting the next time
you need it.
