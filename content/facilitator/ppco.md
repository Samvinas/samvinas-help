# PPCO — for facilitators

PPCO (Pluses, Potentials, Concerns, Overcomes) is the development-phase
workhorse of creative problem solving: a structured, *affirmative*,
**organising** way to strengthen a promising idea instead of debating it.
It's the base of a family — **ALoU**, **LCO** and **PMI** run on the same
engine with different lenses; reach for plain PPCO when you want the full
develop-and-overcome cycle.

## Samvinas

- Pipe in the ideas you want to develop (for example, the top ideas from a
  vote). Each idea becomes a card.
- Participants open a card and add entries to three sections:
  - **Pluses** — what is genuinely good about the idea, right now.
  - **Potentials** — what it might lead to; futures it opens up.
  - **Concerns** — worries, phrased as questions ("How might we…?") so they
    invite answers rather than verdicts.
- Under each concern, the group adds **Overcomes** — concrete ways of
  answering *that specific concern*. Overcomes attach to the concern they
  answer, so the card keeps an honest map of problem → response.
- The four section labels can be renamed per event in the tool's settings —
  useful for translation or house terminology. The order (affirm → imagine →
  question → answer) is the method; keep it.
- One PPCO instance handles many idea cards; you don't need a copy per idea.

PPCO consumes `idea` items and its developed ideas flow downstream — pipe it
into [Voting](/facilitator/voting.html) or
[Ranking](/facilitator/ranking.html) to choose again with clearer eyes.

## Paper and pen

You need: a flip-chart sheet per idea, fat markers, sticky notes, and wall space.

- Head each sheet with the idea, then four columns: **Pluses**, **Potentials**,
  **Concerns**, **Overcomes** — leave the last two side by side.
- Work the columns **in order**, one sticky note per point, said aloud as it
  goes up.
- Concerns go up only as questions ("How might we…?") — rewrite any verdict
  before it's posted.
- Mark the load-bearing concerns with a dot, then take them one at a time:
  each overcome sticky goes directly opposite the concern it answers, so the
  problem → response map survives.
- Failure mode: the group jumping straight to concerns — the sheet becomes a
  complaint list. Hold the order.
- Harvest: photograph each sheet, and write the strengthened idea — with its
  answered concerns — onto a fresh card for the next activity.

## When & why

Reach for PPCO in the **develop** stage — after a focusing tool has picked a
shortlist, before anyone commits. Concerns come *after* the group has
invested in the idea's strengths, so judgement turns constructive, not fatal.

> Affirmative first: strengths before concerns, and one concern at a time.
> The reasoning is on [convergence](/principles/convergence.html).

- Open with the **solution story**: the idea's champion describes it in the
  present tense, as if it's already running — *"What I see us doing is…"*.
  The detail attracts genuine pluses and flushes out the real concerns.
- Insist concerns are questions. "It's too expensive" closes the topic;
  "How might we bring the cost down?" opens it.
- Not every concern needs an overcome — a card is done when every
  load-bearing concern has an answer, or the group has consciously decided
  it can live with it.
- What usually follows: [Voting](/facilitator/voting.html) or
  [Ranking](/facilitator/ranking.html) to re-choose, or
  [Elaboration](/facilitator/elaboration.html) to flesh out the survivor.
