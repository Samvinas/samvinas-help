# Ranking — for facilitators

Ranking is a **convergent** tool that orders items by preference. Unlike voting,
everyone ranks every item, so it shows both the group's order **and** where
opinion is split.

## Samvinas

- Pipe in a short list — ranking works best with roughly 5–10 items; long lists
  are tiring.
- Set the criterion in the **Prompt**.
- Watch the average ranks and the disagreement colouring.

Ranking consumes items from any earlier tool — pipe in the finalists from a
Vote rather than the whole field.

## Paper and pen

You need: the shortlisted items written large on a flip chart and numbered, a
slip of paper per person, and a marker for the tally.

- Read the criterion aloud and write it above the list.
- Everyone writes their own full order on their slip — item numbers, first
  choice at the top — **in silence, before any discussion**.
- Collect the slips and tally on the flip chart: a grid of items × slips, sum
  the positions per item. Lowest total wins.
- The spread is the second result: an item whose positions read 1, 2, 8, 9 has
  a story — mark it.
- One failure mode to avoid: ranking out loud as a group negotiation. The first
  confident voice anchors the order; private slips first, talk after.
- Harvest: write the final order and totals on a fresh sheet and photograph the
  tally grid — the disagreements are worth keeping too.

## When & why

Reach for Ranking when the field is already short and the group needs a
decision-grade order — late in convergence, choosing among finalists.

> A [convergent](/principles/convergence.html) step — and one that shows its
> working: the colouring tells you where the group hasn't actually agreed yet.

- High disagreement on an item is a conversation worth having — surface it.
- Keep the list short. If it's long, **Vote** first to cut it down, then rank
  the finalists.
- What usually follows: developing the winners —
  [Elaboration](/facilitator/elaboration.html) or
  [PPCO](/facilitator/ppco.html) — or an
  [Evaluation Matrix](/facilitator/evaluation-matrix.html) if the choice needs
  several explicit criteria.
