# Elaboration — for facilitators

Elaboration is for depth: participants develop selected ideas with rich text
and sketches. Use it after you've narrowed the field, to turn shortlisted
ideas into fleshed-out proposals — generating detail rather than options.

## Samvinas

- Pipe in the shortlist — connect the **Source** to your focusing tool,
  filtering to (say) the top-voted ideas.
- Set a **Prompt** that guides the deepening: *"What would it take to make
  this work?"*
- Let people choose which ideas to elaborate; **Sign Up** pairs well for
  ownership.
- Export the elaborations for follow-up after the session.

Elaboration consumes `idea` items from any upstream tool; the developed
write-ups — Markdown and sketches — are what you export and carry beyond the
event.

## Paper and pen

You need: one flip-chart sheet per shortlisted idea, fat markers, and wall
space to spread out.

- Head each sheet with one idea from the shortlist; write the guiding prompt
  underneath.
- People sign their name on the sheet they'll develop. Cap it at three or
  four names per sheet — otherwise the favourite drains the room and the rest
  go undeveloped.
- Small groups work at their sheets for 15–20 minutes: who, what, first
  steps, a sketch. Present tense helps — *"what I see us doing is…"*.
- Reconvene with a gallery walk: two minutes per sheet from its authors.
- Harvest: photograph every sheet; the sheets go home with the people who
  signed them.

## When & why

Reach for Elaboration once convergence has done its work — the group holds a
few promising ideas that are still one-liners, and the session's value now
depends on making them concrete. In
[four-stages](/principles/four-stages.html) terms, this is Develop.

- Ownership drives quality: people elaborate hardest on ideas they chose
  themselves.
- Push for specifics — an elaboration without a first step is still a wish.
- What usually follows: [PPCO](/facilitator/ppco.html) to strengthen a
  developed proposal, [Sign Up](/facilitator/signup.html) to attach names to
  actions, or straight to the export and the follow-up meeting.
