# Personal Workspace — for facilitators

Personal Workspace gives every participant their own copy of a mini-flow you
design — for individual reflection, drafting, or practice before coming back
together. It's a structural tool: private, parallel work inside the shared
event.

## Samvinas

- Build the workspace template — the sequence of tools each person gets.
- Participants create and open their own; each is a real, private child
  event, and they move in and out of it with the breadcrumb bar.

The child events hold the work, and nothing flows back into the parent
automatically. To harvest, ask people to bring their best item into a shared
tool when they return — or open a workspace's own options to export its
report if you need what's inside.

## Paper and pen

You need: a printed worksheet per person (or a blank sheet with the prompts
written up on the flip chart) and a pen each.

- Design the sheet as a sequence: prompt at the top, then the steps you'd
  otherwise chain as tools — *three ideas · pick one · one sentence on why*.
- Hand it out, state the timebox, and call silent working time — solo means
  silent, or the quick thinkers colonise the quiet ones' pages.
- People work at their own pace; wander and answer questions quietly rather
  than addressing the room.
- Harvest by invitation: each person shares one item from their sheet, or
  posts a single sticky note to the wall — the sheets themselves stay
  private.
- Failure mode to avoid: collecting the whole sheet. Promise privacy up
  front and keep it — that's what makes the writing honest.

## When & why

Reach for Personal Workspace for "think alone, then share" patterns —
individual work first, group convergence after. Solo thinking produces
genuinely independent starting points and
[widens the field](/principles/divergence.html) before anyone starts
narrowing it.

- Use it when you want everyone to engage deeply, not just the vocal few —
  quiet, deliberate participants often do their best work here.
- What usually follows: a shared pooling step — a
  [Brainstorm](/facilitator/brainstorm.html) where people post their best
  items, or [Clustering](/facilitator/clustering.html) to organise what
  comes back.
