# Find My People — for facilitators

Find My People is a **warm-up** for getting people moving and meeting others.
You ask one question, and everyone picks an answer on their phone. When the
countdown ends, each phone turns the colour of its answer, and the room
physically finds the people who answered the same way.

## Samvinas

- Add it from the **Warm-ups** section of the toolbox. Each step asks **one
  question**, so for a run of five questions add five steps.
- Put the question in the step's **Prompt** (Basics tab). On the **Settings**
  tab, enter the answers one per line (2 to 8) and the **seconds to answer**.
  You can also add an **activity** that appears once the groups have formed,
  e.g. "Share one reason you chose this."
- **Focus the step to start the countdown.** Everyone's phone shows the answers
  as large coloured buttons.
- To stop early, press **End now** on the step's row in the flow list.
- At the reveal, every phone fills with its group's colour, symbol and name
  (● Blue, ▲ Gold, …), so groups can find each other across the room even in
  poor light or with colour-blindness. Anyone who didn't answer becomes a grey
  **Wildcard** and joins any group.
- The projector shows the countdown, then a bar for each group.
- To reuse the step, press **Run again** on its **Data** tab. Focusing a
  finished step again keeps its results.

## Paper and pen

You need: a sign for each answer, taped up in different corners of the room.

- Read the question and the answers aloud, and point to each answer's corner.
- Give people a moment to decide **before** anyone moves. Otherwise they follow
  their friends rather than their answer.
- Say "Go!" and let everyone walk to their corner. Anyone who can't decide
  stands in the middle and joins whichever group is smallest.
- The failure mode is too many answers. Past five or six corners the room
  becomes a scramble, so keep the list short.
- Harvest: count each corner aloud, or take a photograph from the front.

## When & why

Reach for Find My People at the start of a session, or after a break, when you
want people on their feet and talking to someone new.

- **Light questions first.** Start with something easy ("Coffee or tea?")
  before asking anything that reveals a view on the work itself.
- **The groups are the point.** Use the activity prompt to give each group
  something short to do together, so that the movement turns into conversation.
- **Use answers to form working groups.** A question about experience or
  interests ("Which part of the problem do you know best?") builds groups you
  can keep for the next activity.
- What usually follows: a generating tool such as
  [Brainstorm](/facilitator/brainstorm.html), with people staying in their new
  groups, or [Vote With Your Feet](/facilitator/vote-with-your-feet.html) for a
  statement where the spread of opinion matters.
