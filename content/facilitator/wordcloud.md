# Word Cloud — for facilitators

Word Cloud builds a live concordance from the text of piped items, sizing words
by frequency. An **organising** tool — a fast, visual way to surface themes and
shared language.

## Samvinas

- Pipe in a text-rich tool (Brainstorm, Rich Brainstorm).
- Project it on the **Display** for the room.

Word Cloud consumes the text of whatever you pipe into it — the richer the
source, the more the cloud has to say.

## Paper and pen

You need: the idea wall from the previous activity, a flip chart, and a fat
marker.

- Run a **theme tally**: read the notes aloud (or have the group call out),
  and tally-mark each recurring word or phrase on the flip chart as it comes
  round again.
- Fold synonyms into one line as you go — "slow", "delays" and "waiting" are
  one theme, not three. Splitting them is the failure mode: the real pattern
  hides in three small tallies instead of showing in one big one.
- Rewrite the top handful large on a fresh sheet, sized by their counts — a
  hand-drawn cloud the room can react to.
- Harvest: photograph both sheets — the tallies carry the detail, the cloud
  carries the headline.

## When & why

Reach for Word Cloud straight after a generative burst, when you want the room
to see its own language — it arranges words by frequency without judging any of
them.

- Use it as a talking point: "these words keep coming up — what does that tell
  us?"
- It reflects language, not judgement; pair it with a scoring tool when you
  need to prioritise.
- Watch for the word that *isn't* there — the theme everyone expected but
  nobody wrote is worth naming out loud.
- What usually follows: [Clustering](/facilitator/clustering.html) to turn the
  surfaced themes into named groups, or [Voting](/facilitator/voting.html) to
  start converging on the underlying ideas.
