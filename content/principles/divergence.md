# Divergence

Divergence is the widening move of creative work: generating many options —
ideas, questions, interpretations, possibilities — before evaluating any of
them. It is the half that most groups under-do, and the reason is social
rather than intellectual: everyday conversation rewards being right, and
divergence asks you to be *plentiful* instead.

## The four ground rules

1. **Defer judgement.** Nothing is criticised while the field is opening —
   [deferring judgement](/principles/deferring-judgement.html) explains why
   this rule carries the others.
2. **Go for quantity.** The first ideas are the obvious ones; the
   interesting material usually arrives after the group believes it has
   finished. A long list is the raw material good choices are made from.
3. **Welcome wild ideas.** It is easier to tame a wild idea than to
   invigorate a timid one, and the impractical often carries the seed of the
   practical.
4. **Build on the ideas of others.** "Yes, and…" — treat every contribution
   as a springboard rather than a rival.

## What it looks like in Samvinas

The **[generating tools](/facilitator/tools-generating.html)** —
[Brainstorm](/facilitator/brainstorm.html) and its relatives — are built for
exactly this move. Everyone contributes at once, everything is visible, and
nothing is scored yet.

Divergence only earns its keep if it is followed by its partner,
**[convergence](/principles/convergence.html)**.
