# Importer

A reference list of ideas your facilitator brought into the event — the
starting material everyone is working from.

## What you can do

- Read the list. Each idea keeps its number, so it's easy to refer to
  "number 12" out loud.
- That's it — this list is read-only. The activities built on it (voting,
  clustering, developing) happen in the event's other tools, and anything
  new you think of belongs in a brainstorm.

Most of the time you won't see this tool at all: facilitators usually keep
it tucked away and let its ideas appear inside the activity you're doing.
