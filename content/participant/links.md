# Links

A shared list of useful web pages. Add the ones you know about, and open the
ones others have found.

## How to add a link

1. **Title:** a few words that say what the page is, such as *"2024 survey
   results"*. If you leave it blank, the site's name is used instead.
2. **Web address:** paste the address from your browser. You can leave off the
   `https://`; Samvinas adds it.
3. Choose **Add**. Your link appears in the list straight away, for everyone.

If the address doesn't look right, Samvinas says so under the box and keeps
what you typed, so you can fix it.

## Opening a link

Choose any title in the list. It opens in a new tab or window, so you won't
lose your place in the session.

> A good title saves everyone a click. Say what's useful about the page, not
> just its name.

## Changing your mind

To remove a link you added, open its **⋯** menu and choose **Delete link**.
You'll get a moment to undo it.
