# Soapbox

Volunteer for a short talk to the whole group — usually two minutes, no
slides — about anything you think the group would find interesting.

## How to take part

- Enter your name, and optionally a topic, then tap **Add me to the list**.
- Your card shows your place in the queue; it updates as the list moves.
- Changed your mind? Tap **Withdraw** — that only works from this device, so
  if you're elsewhere just ask the facilitator to take you off.
- Crossed-out names below the queue have already spoken.

## Tips

- A topic line helps the facilitator pick a varied set of talks.
- Slots are limited — being on the list doesn't guarantee a slot, but the
  queue carries over to the next soapbox session.
