# Scenario Generator

You will build scenarios in two steps. First everyone collects ideas under a
few headings, such as *Character* and *Location*. Then Samvinas combines one
idea from each heading into a scenario for you to explore.

## How to take part

**Collecting ideas**

- You see the list of headings, each with a question and a count of the ideas
  so far.
- Tap **Brainstorm** beside a heading to open its page. Type an idea and tap
  **Add**. You can see everyone's ideas there, newest first.
- Tap **Back to the list** and move on to the next heading. Try to add
  something to every one.
- You can edit or delete your own ideas from the ⋯ beside them.

**Making scenarios**

- When the facilitator moves the group on, the screen changes by itself.
- Tap **New scenario**. Samvinas draws one idea from each heading and shows
  them together on a card. Tap again for a different combination.
- Everyone's cards are shown, with yours at the top.
- Under each card is a box. Write what happens in that scenario, then tap
  **Add**. You can respond to anyone's card, not only your own.
- You can delete a scenario you made, and edit or delete your own responses,
  from the ⋯ menu.

**In a breakout group**

- There is an **Ideas / Scenarios** switch at the top. Move between the two
  whenever you like. Switching only changes your own screen, so you won't
  interrupt anyone else in the group.

## Tips

- **Go for quantity while collecting.** The more ideas under each heading,
  the more varied the scenarios.
- **Add the odd ones.** An unusual idea often makes the most interesting
  scenario.
- **Work with the combination you are given.** A pairing that looks wrong is
  the point: ask what would have to be true for it to make sense.
- **Build on other people's cards.** A second response to someone's scenario
  can take it somewhere they hadn't thought of.
