# Vote With Your Feet

Show where you stand on a statement by placing yourself on a line between two
ends.

## How to take part

- If the facilitator asks, enter your name and choose your role. Your phone
  remembers them for the rest of the event, and **Change** lets you update them.
- Move the slider to the point on the line that matches your view. It saves as
  you move, and you can keep adjusting until voting closes.
- If you're undecided, press **I'm in the middle**.
- While voting is open, the projector shows dots without names.
- When the facilitator closes voting, the projector shows where everyone stood,
  with names if the facilitator has turned them on.

## Tips

- Place yourself honestly, not where you think the room will be. The spread is
  the useful part.
- Be ready to say a sentence about why you stood where you did.
- Everyone can see your name at the reveal unless the facilitator has turned
  names off.
