# Find My People

Answer one question, then find the other people who answered the same way.

## How to take part

- When the countdown starts, tap your answer. You get one choice, so take a
  moment before you tap.
- Your phone fills with your answer's colour while you wait for the others.
- When the countdown ends, your phone shows your group's **colour, symbol and
  name**. Hold it up and find others showing the same one.
- If you didn't answer in time, you're a **Wildcard**: join any group you like.
- **Hide colour** gives you your normal screen back, and **Show my colour**
  brings the colour back.

## Tips

- Go with your first instinct. It's a warm-up, not a test.
- Look around the room as well as at your phone. Holding phones up makes the
  groups easier to spot.
- If the facilitator has given your group something to do, it appears under
  your colour.
