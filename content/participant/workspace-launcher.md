# Personal Workspace

Your own private mini-activity, built from a template the facilitator set up.
Everyone gets their own.

## How to take part

- Open your workspace to start; it's yours to work through at your own pace.
- Use the breadcrumb bar to move in and out of it.

## Tips

- Take your time — other participants can't see your workspace. When the group
  reconvenes you'll usually be invited to share your best item; the rest stays
  yours. (The facilitator can open a workspace's report if needed.)
